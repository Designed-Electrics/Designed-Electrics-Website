Plumbing & gas at Designed Electrics

One team for the wiring, the water and the gas.

Most jobs in a home or business touch more than one trade. A new hot water system needs a plumber, a gas fitter and an electrician. A kitchen renovation needs new outlets, a cooktop connection and fresh circuits. We handle all three under one booking, so there is one point of contact, one schedule and one set of compliance paperwork at the end.

Quality by design

Good work is planned before the tools come out. We map every pipe run, gas line and circuit first, so the job is done right the first time and built to last.

Our licensed plumbers and gas fitters cover general repairs, fixture installs, appliance connections, gas line extensions and leak detection across Perth and regional WA. Every job is tested before we leave, and you receive the certificates your insurer, builder or landlord will ask for.

Why choose us
