At handover

Home backup - EPS

When the grid goes down, the battery keeps the essential circuits running. This is how the board is set up for it.

Three mains Separate main switches for the grid, the inverter and the alternative supply.
Changeover One switch moves the backup circuits between battery supply and grid, marked up and down.
Array data Array locations, open circuit voltage and short circuit current written on the board.
Signposted Inverter, battery and isolation switch locations marked for anyone who opens the door.
